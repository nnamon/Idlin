Measured with `camera.test.ts`, seed 1, `main` then this change. A pan "with
a reason" is one whose subject had a haul, a fight or a down in the 15
seconds before the view set out.

- Fifteen residents, three game days: 14 pans (0.2 a game hour), 8 with a
  reason; then 30 (0.4 a game hour), 23 with a reason and 7 leaving a region
  nobody was left in. With monsters: 10, 7 with a reason; then 20, 17 with a
  reason and 3 leaving an emptied region.
- Six residents, three game days: 22 pans, 9 with a reason; then 21 plus 2
  that turned back, every one with a reason or leaving an emptied region. Out
  and straight back: 4, then 2, both following a resident who walked home a
  few seconds after the view arrived. No pan comes to an empty region.
