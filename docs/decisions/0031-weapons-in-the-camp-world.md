# 0031: Weapons and the flask in the camp world

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #393, slice piece 6, open to veto. Section 3.1
of [slice.md](../slice.md) gives the four weapon families' blows, times,
hits and knockback, and section 5 the flask. They are built in
`packages/sim/src/weapons.ts` and the world, which is still the camp and its
regions with no gear. These choices were made where the spec was silent or
the camp world got in the way.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                           | Rejected alternative                                         | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A blow faces one of eight directions, arrows included, and its tiles are fixed by that direction.                                                                                                  | Arrows flying a drawn line straight at the aimed tile.       | The marks a program dodges are then one fixed shape, the same in the wind-up and in flight, and a snapshot can check them against the actor's aim. A bow is still hard to dodge at nine tiles; a program lines up on a row, column or diagonal to use it. |
| An arrow flies a tile a tick.                                                                                                                                                                      | Two tiles a tick, or landing at once.                        | At a tile a tick a monster six tiles off has six ticks to step off the line, about one of its steps, so an arrow can miss one that moved, as the issue asks.                                                                                              |
| Every resident holds a blade; a `spawn` input can name another family. Melee stands in for Arms in the times, as it does for the blow (0030).                                                      | Choosing a weapon by `!wear` now.                            | Wearing gear is piece 13. The input lets tests and captures set up every family today.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A body knocked back in its wind-up takes its blow with it, aimed the same way from where it lands. Each actor keeps its `aim`.                                                                     | Knockback breaking the wind-up off.                          | Breaking a wind-up is the stagger's job (4.2, piece 7), with its own line and lockout. Keeping the aim also lets a snapshot check every wind-up's marks exactly.                                                                                          |
| A version 9 snapshot loads with a blade and a full flask for each resident. A resident's wind-up from then is called off and its recovery cut to a blade's 3, after the version 9 hash is checked. | Refusing a version 9 snapshot that has a resident mid-swing. | A saved world is kept whenever it can be; the version 9 blow does not fit a blade, and a fresh decision a tick later is all it costs.                                                                                                                     |
| The wolf's hitpoints go from 42 to 101, 12/5 of what they were. The goblin keeps 14.                                                                                                               | Leaving the wolf alone, or scaling the goblin too.           | A blade's blow comes every 5 ticks where the old one came every 12. With 42 hitpoints the standing program won all 100 duels, the same as the dodging one. A goblin runs once it is half down, after one blow at 14; at 34 it would take three.           |
| The flask is full again after a down, at camp.                                                                                                                                                     | Only kills refilling it until there is a deck.               | slice.md fills it aboard; camp is where a downed resident goes until the deck is built (piece 2).                                                                                                                                                         |
| "Faster" lines and the carry between blows are in `blowTimes`, which takes and returns the carry, but nobody keeps a carry yet.                                                                    | A carry on every actor now.                                  | Nothing can be worn, so the carry would always be 0. It goes on the citizen with gear.                                                                                                                                                                    |
