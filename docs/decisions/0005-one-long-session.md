# 0005: One long session, and merging as one UTC-dated commit

Date: 2026-11-26. Decided by the conductor after the operator said the session
should keep going until told to pause. Replaces the three-hour cadence in
row 13 of [0001](0001-founding-decisions.md).

| Decision                                                                                                                                                       | Rejected alternative                                                   | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The conductor works in one long session, one issue after another. The three-hourly schedule stays as a fallback that runs only when no session holds the lock. | A cycle every three hours and nothing in between.                      | Most of a three-hour slot was spent idle. The operator asked for continuous work.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| While a pull request is with its reviewer, the conductor may start the next issue that doesn't depend on it, on its own branch from `main`.                    | Waiting for each review; or stacking branches.                         | Reviews take several minutes each and come in rounds. Stacked branches have to be untangled after a squash.                                                                                                                                                     |
| At most two child tasks at once, and one heavy container job (a capture or a gate run) at a time.                                                              | One child task at a time; or no limit.                                 | A reviewer and a watch judge don't depend on each other. Capture and gate runs are both heavy.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| A branch is squashed locally with `TZ=UTC`, and once `gate` has passed on that commit it is pushed to `main` as a fast-forward.                                | GitHub's squash or rebase merge, with or without a `Time-Zone` header. | Both methods rewrite the commit and write one of its dates in the account's time zone (squash the author date, rebase the committer date), and the repo is meant to carry nothing about where it is developed. A fast-forward leaves the commit as it was made. |

A session takes the lock once and keeps it until it ends, rewriting its time
as it goes, so the schedule does not start a second cycle during a long review. If the daily watch review is skipped
because the lock was held, the session runs it between cycles.
