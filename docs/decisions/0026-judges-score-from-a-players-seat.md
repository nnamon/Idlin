# 0026: Judges score from a player's seat, at 15 residents

Date: 2026-11-27. Decided for #314, open to veto. The operator asked why the
playtesters had not raised an oversized page that listed six of twelve
residents, and stats that meant nothing. The judges had seen both: "thirty
identical 1s are noise", "a seventh resident would not fit". Each said it as
an idea for later, because each was asked whether a change was better than
`main`, and a page bad on both sides scores "same". The captures ran six
residents, the number the page was built for.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Rejected alternative                                                              | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Every watch judgement adds an absolute verdict from the seat of a viewer who owns one resident among 15: can I find mine in five seconds, can I tell how it is doing, would I keep watching. Each is yes or no, and any "no" is a fix-now `watch` issue, in the change's scope or not. | Only the comparison with `main`, with absolute problems filed as ideas for later. | A comparison can't see a problem that is on both sides. The questions are the ones a player would ask, and a yes or no can't be filed behind polish the way a score of 3 can.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A fix-now issue raised a second time (a `[conductor] raised again:` comment) holds every `look` merge until it is closed, except a pull request that closes one such issue.                                                                                                            | Fix-now issues as ordinary queue items with no hold.                              | Without a hold, a problem a player notices every day can wait behind new features indefinitely. Two independent judgements are enough to say it isn't one judge's taste.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| The daily watch review is a playtest: an open-ended list of what that player would complain about, worst first, kept newest first in `playtest.md` on `media`. It gives no scores.                                                                                                     | Daily scores against the rubric's seven dimensions in `watch-log.md`.             | A score is a summary that leaves out what to fix, and the judges had put what a player would notice under ideas for later. A list of complaints asks for exactly those.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Captures run 15 residents by default, with one full-size frame at six (`residents-6.png`). Hard fails are judged at 15 and at six.                                                                                                                                                     | Six residents with crowd sheets at 12, 50 and 150.                                | Six fit the page exactly, so nothing about a crowd showed at full size; the crowd sheets are small grids read at thumbnail size. 15 is the crowd the plan's next milestones are shown at.                                                                                                                                                     |
| The dead-air metric counts a change in a listed resident's page line (as `line.ts` builds it) as visible, and a change of the rule in charge alone as not.                                                                                                                             | Keep counting rule changes.                                                       | Since the page line comes from the resident's state, a rule change shows on the page only if it changes the line. A slip's glyph does show a rule change on the map, but only for the few residents with a slip, so it is left out. At seed 1 dead air over a game day is 2.6 s at six residents and 1.8 s at 15, well inside the 20 s limit. |
| The fight sheets, the figures sheet and the burst find their moments by searching a headless run of the same world: the first resident downed with a kill within 70 ticks; the moment from 07:00 to 09:00 with most residents on the map; the busiest two ticks after 07:00.           | Fixed hours.                                                                      | Each fixed hour went stale when the world changed: the wolf was already dead at the fight hour, the figures frame showed one resident in a forest, and the burst was twelve identical frames at 06:00.                                                                                                                                        |

Checked for this: the searches and the page-line metric have tests in
`tools/capture/src/simrun.test.ts` and `metrics.test.ts`, and one `--review`
capture on the branch. Not checked: whether 15 residents is still the right
seat once the page lists everyone (#333).
