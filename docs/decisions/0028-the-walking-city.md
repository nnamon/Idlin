# 0028: The walking city

Date: 2026-11-27. Decided by the design lead on
[#331](https://github.com/nnamon/Idlin/issues/331); open to veto.

The operator turned down the plan of decisions 0022 and 0023 and proposed a
machine city on legs that crawls the world in cycles: travel, land, go out,
get back before it leaves. The conductor adopted the direction, gave a first
take, and then put five ideas of its own beside the operator's, of which the operator's rulings removed three. This note
records the choices made in working all of it out. The game is in
[plan.md](../plan.md) and the vision in [design.md](../design.md).

It supersedes 0003 (one frame and one map size), 0017 (regions), 0020 (the
view follows the spotlight), 0022 and 0023. The numbers of 0022 for blows,
hitpoints, monster ranks and experience stand.

## The conductor's five ideas

| Idea                         | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Rejected part                                                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The language is the loot     | Rejected. Every sensor and action is open to every program from a resident's first minute.                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The whole idea: a starting vocabulary of 16 words, the rest found as pages.                            | A draft of the plan adopted it and the operator turned it down. The game is built on writing a program, and locking sensors and actions behind drops makes every new resident's program worse than its author. A build is skills, kit and the program. |
| The horde is the clock       | Adopted. No countdown. The city lifts when the horde's front reaches its lamps, between 10 and 15 minutes in. The front stops where it is being killed. The horde's lead behind the city, 0 to 6, is earned and lost and sets the next landing's length. It is one horde for a whole crossing. | A cycle of no fixed length.                                                                            | Set-down stays at :00, :20 and :40, so a stream has a minute to come back for and two cities are in step. Only the lift moves. A horde that can be slowed makes the deadline something residents fight over, which a timer cannot be.                  |
| Left behind is a story       | Rejected. A resident not aboard at the lift is winched in, and its bag and worn kit stay on the ground.                                                                                                                                                                                        | The whole idea: the stranded surviving, or walking after the city, in a window of their own.           | A draft adapted it and the operator turned it down. The world moves round the city so that everyone is always on one screen and the camera never goes anywhere else.                                                                                   |
| The expedition draws the map | Adapted. The land is inked outward from the city as the frame pulls back at set-down, and the whole landing is known from then. The plate records what happened on it and is kept.                                                                                                             | Residents inking the map as they walk, with programs knowing only inked ground, which a draft adopted. | The operator's account of the zoom puts the drawing in the reveal. A landing's size and what is on it are what the choice of stop is about, so they are shown.                                                                                         |
| Chat plays the threat        | Dropped. The horde's own program decides where it comes from and what it sends.                                                                                                                                                                                                                | The whole idea: viewers without a resident voting for the horde.                                       | The operator's rule is that one chatter is one player, so there are no viewers without a resident, and a chatter voting against their own city is on no side. Threats come from the world, rival cities, and later other streams.                      |

| A landing's size is its difficulty, in four classes: clearing (80 by 45 tiles, 24 px a tile, 150 monsters, no core), glade, vale, and valley (192 by 108, 10 px, 1,200 monsters, two cores, a larger and earlier horde). | No accounting of seconds on screen, and no guarantee per owner. Banners and legend entries go first to chatters who are present.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The 240 seconds an hour of 0023, and the 1,200 an earlier draft promised.                                                                                                         | Nobody needs a share of a frame that holds everyone.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Ten modules.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Upgrades bought with goods alone; a vote on what to build.                                                                                                                        | A core has to be seen coming home, and carrying it costs the carrier their own haul.                                                                                                                                                                             |

| A gate is the one landing where the city cannot leave: it is forced by eight minutes of work while everyone else holds the lamps against the horde.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | A gate as a boss to kill.                                                                                                                                                         | Every other landing ends by running.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| A city is never destroyed. With the hull at no hitpoints it founders for a cycle and one module loses a tier.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Losing the city.                                                                                                                                                                  | It holds a month of everyone's work.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| A crossing of the continent, about four weeks, ends with a new continent, a new horde, the small hull and skills at 1. | One chatter is one player. A first line in chat makes a resident named by the chat username, which comes up through a hatch in every phase. There are no house residents. Until there is a real chat, a scripted crowd of usernames sends the world lines as a chat would.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `!claim` of an existing resident; a join command; bot residents to fill a city.                                                                                                   | The operator's rule. Their words on claiming: "Feels like shit to get sloppy seconds of a bot."                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Joining, commands, quiet chatters and votes are chat.md's (decision 0027). This plan sets what they reach: berths come from the hull, 40 to 150; a landing's counts are quartered for a cast of 5 and under and halved up to 25; a city with nobody awake walks on dormant and loses nothing; chat.md's three phases are given their lengths; the vote on the next stop is drawn on the route map opened on the plate. Chatters have no page or editor for now, and a resident runs a preset.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | This plan's own rules for quiet residents (aboard after 3 days, off after 14) and its companion page and editor, which earlier drafts had.                                        | chat.md landed with the operator's scope: chatters steer through chat alone. | One streamer is one city, with the stream's name and colours on it from M1. From M1 everything in the world carries a city id, a stop's map comes from the continent's seed and position alone, valleys have a second place to park, and the frame is fitted to a list of cities. | A raid on a published copy of the rival, which a draft had; one server hosting both cities; designing it when it is reached.                                                      | The operator: competing machine cities "would be sick". A resident's shot that meets another resident is spent and shoves them, and does no harm.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Tile-adjacent blows only; shots that hurt friends, as #332 was filed.                                                                                                             | The operator asked for physics attacks and many more packs. Checked for this: the core costs against the gates' ratings at 45 cores a
day; that each size of landing fits a 16:9 frame at its tile size, and each hull four fifths of one. Not checked: anything on screen, any rate of residents winched in, whether a fight reads at 10 px a tile,
whether a front can be held for a believable time, and a tick with monsters
awake in hundreds. M1 measures all four.
