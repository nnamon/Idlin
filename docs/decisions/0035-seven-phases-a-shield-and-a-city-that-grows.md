# 0035: Seven phases, a shield, and a city that grows

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #413, open to veto. The operator, on the
landing: "it would be nice to see the sense of scale of the map and the city
as they grow… Things should grow to epic proportions after significant
investment in the game", and seven phases for a landing: peace, scouts, the
front gathering at the edges, the march, a shield bubble the horde slams
and citizens can fight to hold, the break, and the horde forcing the city
to leave. The rules are in [slice.md](../slice.md), sections 1, 2 and 9.
What is drawn of them is [art.md](../art.md)'s.

**The numbers.** `packages/sim/scripts/plate.ts --growth` prints the size
table from stand-in maps of every size, `--pace` the level table with and
without the siege, the siege's drops and the works' default order, and
`--siege` how long a shield holds ([0036](0036-no-clock-on-the-shield-and-a-growing-horde.md)).
At shield 3 the siege pays the hour-10 citizen on untouched settings 10
monsters a landing, worth 13 experience on its share's 180 and 0.038 rares
on its 0.64.

**What is not checked.** Nothing here was run in the simulation. The works'
pace takes 10 ore a citizen a landing, which is a guess until piece 32
counts it. How long a shield lasts with real citizens fighting, at 15 and at
150, is piece 31's measurement. City sizes 3 and 4 are a plan: no map over
80 by 45 can be generated, and no plate over 700 monsters has been timed.
