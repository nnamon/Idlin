# 0034: Packs laid in pockets

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #391, open to veto. The operator, on the look
target: the landing should be "more like a rpg map", the horde "more of a
time pressure thing", with "pockets of resources and packs of enemies to pay
attention to when exploring the randomly generated map". Section 1 of
[slice.md](../slice.md) had put most of a landing's fighting into monsters
sent to each citizen out of the ground near it, so the map held little worth
walking to. This note replaces the fourth row of decision 0029. What the
horde does is in [0035](0035-seven-phases-a-shield-and-a-city-that-grows.md).

**What changed in the numbers.** In plan.md's level table each level comes
up to 9% sooner than with sent monsters (level 23 at 9.4 hours where it was
9.8, level 40 at 32 where it was 35), and hours 1, 10 and 100 still give
levels 6, 23 and 58 for a citizen paid its whole share. On the second city's
medium landing section 1's runs, on stand-ins, pay every tier of the crowd
89% or more at 15 awake and 84% or more at 150, with the tier between the
median and the strongest at Arms 30, 40 or 50. The drop table in section
3.2 moves by 8% or less in every row but the two T1 rows: the hour-100
citizen finds a rare with a T1 line by its share's drops every 10.5 hours
where it was 4.4, because only the tenth of its share at 68 and its two
rolls there can roll one. The bench still gives it one every 2 hours from a
level-65 base.

**How it was checked.** `packages/sim/scripts/plate.ts` lays the shares by
these rules onto 100 landings of each size and prints section 1's numbers.
It builds stand-ins with the yard as the bubble and open, as #415 and #416
will have the generator cut them, and `--generator` lays the generator's 80
by 45 map as it is today. `--middle` moves the tier between the median and
the strongest. `--pace` reproduces the level table and the drop table. Piece
5 measures the same things in the simulation. Nothing was run to show that a
tier kills what was laid for it in the time, beyond section 6's count of
swings.
