# 0041: Monsters on a landing, in the simulation

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided on #447 (slice piece 5), open to veto. The landing's
packs, shares and dens of [slice.md](../slice.md), section 1, are laid at
legs-plant and run in the simulation (`packages/sim/src/laying.ts` and
`packs.ts`).

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                 | Rejected                                                                             | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A laid monster runs no Tactics program; its pack's brain is the simulation's own.                                                                                                        | A shipped monster program with sensors for the post and the pack's target.           | The rules that matter (keep to the pocket, leave alone a citizen ten levels over, the whole pack goes for one citizen) have to hold whatever a program says, and 600 to 700 of them decide at 150 citizens. |

| Up to three laid fodder share a tile (plan.md, section 2.2).                                                                                                                             | A swarm on a bigger square.                                                          | A swarm is laid on its post and the eight tiles round it, which the post rules are worked out for.                                                                                                          |
