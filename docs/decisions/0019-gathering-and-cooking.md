# 0019: Gathering, cooking and eating

Date: 2026-11-27. Made in #236, skills part 2.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                          | Rejected alternative                                                  | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trees and fishing spots are listed in each map file under `nodes`, by kind and tile. The kinds (`tree`, `old_tree`, `fishing_spot`, `deep_pool`) are a fixed list in the simulation.              | Every `tree` and `water` tile is a node; node kinds as content files. | The camp has dozens of tree tiles and a whole river: if every one gave goods, nothing would run out where anyone could see it. Programs name kinds by number, so their ids must never move, as with goods and system calls. |
| A node gives a fixed number of goods, then stands empty for a fixed time and comes back full. Its state is two numbers: goods left, and the tick it is due back.                                  | A chance per good of running out; rolling the yield when it regrows.  | A fixed count is easy to read off the stream and to test, and costs no roll.                                                                                                                                                |
| One `chop` or `fish` is one try with odds from the level (25 in 100 at the level the node needs, up to 90), and the actor then stands 12 ticks.                                                   | One action that runs until a good comes, or until the bag is full.    | A program gets to decide again a little more than once a second, so a monster arriving or a hit taken is answered between tries, and `eat` works in the middle of a fight.                                                  |
| Better nodes need a level (15) and give more experience per good, but the same goods.                                                                                                             | A good per tier (oak logs, eels).                                     | More goods would mean more bank columns and more snapshot steps for nothing the game uses yet.                                                                                                                              |
| The node sensors look only in the actor's own region, skip nodes its level does not allow, and give the nearest free tile beside a node rather than the node's own tile.                          | Look across the whole world; give the node's tile.                    | Enemies are only seen in one's own region, and nodes follow that. `walk_to` cannot walk onto a tree, and a free side spreads several gatherers round one node instead of sending them all to one tile.                      |
| A burnt fish is a good of its own, `burnt_fish`, that cannot be eaten. Cooking uses a log as well as the fish.                                                                                    | A burnt fish disappears.                                              | A burnt fish in the bag and the bank is something a viewer can count. The log makes the woodcutters' goods worth something to the fishers, ahead of the fire burning logs (#301).                                           |
| Cooking works within two steps of a campfire tile.                                                                                                                                                | Only beside it, as banking is beside the chest.                       | The camp fire has four tiles beside it. A cook sent to the `fire` place while it was taken ended on a tile two steps off and could not cook.                                                                                |
| Snapshots are version 9. Version 8 is read with every node full and a 0 count of burnt fish in every bank; a version 8 bag or bank holding a burnt fish or the wrong number of counts is refused. | Refuse version 8.                                                     | The world server resumes from its snapshot. A map with nodes hashes differently from one without, so in practice a version 8 snapshot only comes back on ground with no nodes.                                              |

The viewer does not yet draw a node that has run out, a resident chopping or
fishing, or a haul; that is #238.
