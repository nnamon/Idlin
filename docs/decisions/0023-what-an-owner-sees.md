# 0023: What an owner sees as the world grows

Date: 2026-11-27. Decided by the design lead after a
[fun audit](https://github.com/nnamon/Idlin/pull/325#issuecomment-7219490742)
of the plan; open to veto. The operator had asked whether each milestone was
evaluated for why it is fun, and warned that the more the world expands, the
less of the player it shows. The audit found that the plan's 14 regions left
an owner's resident in the map frame for about 190 seconds an hour, a quarter
of what four regions give, and that nothing in the plan worked that out. The
operator then asked for the map to be zoomed out (#326), which changes the
arithmetic again. [plan.md](../plan.md) sections 4.5 and 4.6 are the result.
