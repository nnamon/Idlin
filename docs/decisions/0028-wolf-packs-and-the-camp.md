# 0028: Wolves hunt in packs, more monsters come out, and the camp is not a safe zone

Date: 2026-11-27. Made in #298, wolves hunt, after the operator said a lone
wolf at the fire was no danger to anyone, and then that monster density was
not there at all.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Rejected alternative                                                                                   | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Night wolves come as a pack of three to five. `night_group` in a monster file may be a list of two, the fewest and the most, and the number is drawn from the world's generator when the group comes out. By day they come in twos and threes: `group` may be such a list too.                                                                                                                     | Pairs by night, as #296 asked first; a fixed pack of four.                                             | A pair was easy to outnumber and read as two strays. A drawn size makes some nights worse than others.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Residents no longer count toward spawning: the crowd target of one actor per 25 walkable tiles (#271) is gone. A region's cap counts monsters only and grows with the residents in it: by day 4, plus 1 for every 3 residents, up to 12; by night 10, plus 1 for every 2, up to 24. At six residents that is 6 by day and 13 by night. All of it is `MONSTER_CAPS` in `packages/sim/src/world.ts`. | Keep the crowd target and raise the flat caps of 3 by day and 6 by night.                              | The camp holds most of the residents, so under the crowd target it was nearly always full and spawned almost nothing: on `main`, seeds 1 to 5 at six residents averaged 1.6 monsters there by night and had none out for 22% of the night. A busy region should draw more monsters. The operator then asked for more packs than caps of half these numbers gave (2 + 1 per 3 by day, 5 + 1 per 2 by night, up to 6 and 12): at six residents they averaged 3.4 monsters by day and 6.7 by night, and these average 4.9 and 11.5. The ceiling of 24 keeps 150 residents from bringing hundreds. |
| A group's size stays limited by day to 3 and by night to 6 (`MOST_IN_GROUP`, `MOST_IN_NIGHT_GROUP`). A pack comes in as many as were drawn or as there is room for, whichever is fewer, and not at all with room for fewer than its fewest.                                                                                                                                                        | Let a group be as big as the cap.                                                                      | Groups are content; the caps are how many groups a region holds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| The wait between groups in a region is 60 ticks, six game minutes, instead of a game hour. A snapshot may still hold a wait of up to a game hour, as saved before.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | A game hour.                                                                                           | A region now refills within a few game minutes of a fight. At six residents a monster was out for all but 0.4% of the night.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Monsters may walk onto the camp's ground. They still never come out on it and can't be put there by an input. Snapshots no longer refuse a monster standing there. This reverses part of 0011, and replaces the "Camp ground" row of the settled decisions in `docs/plan.md`, which kept the ground closed.                                                                                        | Keep the ground closed and let a pack strike onto it from its edge.                                    | Measured over ten game days with the ground closed, packs went into the light 18 to 100 times a day but downed nobody there: the residents sleep round the fire, out of a 2-tile reach from the edge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| By night a wolf goes into the light when its pack (itself and the wolves within ten tiles, `friends_near(10)`) outnumbers the residents in the light (`lit_enemies`, a new sensor). Outnumbered, it walks out and paces at the edge. By day it keeps out regardless. `friends_near` and `threat_friends` count an actor's own kind only.                                                           | Packs go into the light whenever they like; any hour. Counting every monster as pack.                  | The operator asked for this rule. A camp that holds more residents than the pack is still safe, so sitting together at the fire is worth something. With goblins counted as pack, a lone wolf near three goblin trios could go in against up to nine residents, which reads as the rule not working.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Goblins follow the same rule: by night a goblin group that outnumbers the residents in the light goes in after them, and one that doesn't keeps out of it; by day they keep out. A goblin in the light with no prey there walks out.                                                                                                                                                               | Goblins always keep out of the light.                                                                  | The watch judge saw goblins standing in the light beside hurt residents doing nothing, in the same camp where wolves were downing residents. One rule for every monster at the fire is easier to read. Out of a fight goblins roam a round of places, every goblin heading for the same place in the same half game hour, so a group keeps together without state of its own; the operator asked that they stop standing at the den.                                                                                                                                                           |
| A resident mending at camp after a down cannot be struck: blows pass it by, and monsters' sensors leave it out. It is the state a down leaves (waiting, no rule in charge, no fault), read by `mending` in `packages/sim/src/world.ts`, so the snapshot format does not change.                                                                                                                    | A new field on each actor and a snapshot version; or leaving the limit to the wolf program.            | Without it, a pack downed a lone resident again while it still lay there: 7 to 43 times in two game days, by the review's count. | A wolf has had its fill of the camp once it has bitten three times at prey in the light, or once anyone lies mending there (`lit_mending`, a new sensor). Then it walks out and keeps out of the light for a game hour. Every wolf in the region sees the resident mending, so a pack leaves after its first down in the light. A goblin that sees someone mending keeps out for a game hour too.  | Three bites for each wolf, renewed at each place on its round; or a count of bites kept for each pack. | Three bites each let a pack of five down a resident again and again, and all but one place on the round lie within ten tiles of the light's edge, so the renewal brought the pack straight back. A count for each pack needs state a pack doesn't have. One down a visit, a game hour apart, is what the hunt test holds: over a night with wolves at the caps above, a resident alone by the fire was downed 10 times in each of seeds 1 to 5, and never twice within half a game hour.                                                                                                       |
| Back off, stalk and commit compare the prey's group with the pack: back off when the group is two or more bigger, stalk when it is one bigger, hunt otherwise.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The fixed numbers of #296 (back off from three, stalk two, hunt one).                                  | The fixed numbers only made sense for a pair. For a lone wolf the two rules agree.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Residents are downed far more often than before: over seeds 1 to 5, about 72
times a game day at six residents against 4 on `main`, and 140 at fifteen.
Nearly all of those are out on the map. A down still costs only fifteen
seconds of rest at camp; #237 makes it cost more.

The caps and the wait are a step towards the budgets of section 3.4 of
[docs/plan.md](../plan.md), which #316 puts in place of them.
