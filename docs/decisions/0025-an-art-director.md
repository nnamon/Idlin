# 0025: An art director, and an art bible

Date: 2026-11-27. Decided by the conductor on #334, after the operator said:
"I think you need an art director in there too for artistic vision. Currently
art feels like an afterthought tbh. It's very rough." The bible was written by
the first art director (Mythos 5.2) from an audit of the captures of #330 and
#333, which is posted on #334.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                  | Rejected alternative                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A new role, the art director (Mythos 5.2, a fresh child each time), owns the look and `docs/art.md`.                                                                                      | Leaving art direction to the watch judge, as section 2 of `AGENTS.md` had it. | The judge scores a result against `main` without knowing what was meant. Nobody decided beforehand how a thing should be drawn, so each builder drew its part its own way and the parts don't match. |
