# 0029: The slice, its loot loop and its damage rules

Date: 2026-11-27. Decided for #365, under #361, open to veto. The operator
set the game on a walking city (#331), stopped the widening of the camp
prototype (#361), and asked for loot as the spine of the loop and for firm
damage rules. [slice.md](../slice.md) is the spec. It replaces the
milestones of [plan.md](../plan.md), which now keeps only the arithmetic the
slice is built on, and decisions 0022 to 0024 no longer describe the plan.

An adversarial critic (Mythos 5.2) read the draft before the pull request,
over five passes. The review on the pull request (Mythos 5.2) then found six
gaps: the strongest citizens were not paid, a full stash broke up what its
chatter wanted, two tests could not pass as written, three rules had to be
guessed, an away chatter could lose worn gear in two ways, and the files
disagreed. The rows from "Ground has a level" to "The winch answers" settle
them. A second critic (Mythos 5.2) read the result once and found holes in
three of the fixes, which were closed without another pass. The operator also ruled out
ornate names, so tools, items and features are named for what they do.
