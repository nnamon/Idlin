# 0037: The agreement after the turn to the slice

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #366, on the operator's instructions there and
in the threads since #361. Changes sections 1 to 6, 8 and 10 of `AGENTS.md`,
steps 3, 4, 6, 7 and 10 of `process/cycle.md` and its limits, step 2 and the
digest of `process/daily.md`, `docs/watch-rubric.md` and one line of
`docs/roadmap.md`. The team had been working this way since #361 was filed;
this writes it down.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Rejected alternative                                                                                                                              | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The current milestone is one walking-city cycle at finished quality (#361). Breadth waits, and camp-game issues from before #361 are parked without `ready`.                                                                                                                                      | Building the slice alongside the camp backlog, lowest issue number first.                                                                         | Widening the camp game left most of its screen short of art.md, as roadmap.md records. One cycle done well shows what the whole game has to reach.                                                                                                          |
| The operator's picture of the game is written into section 1. The conductor checks it before a brief and before merging a spec or `look` pull request, and a drift from it blocks like a bug.                                                                                                     | Relying on design.md, slice.md and art.md alone, each reviewed piece by piece.                                                                    | Piece-by-piece review let the work drift from what the operator described while each piece passed; the landing turning into a left-to-right lane is the case that led to this. A short list checked at the two points where drift gets in catches it early. |
| A new or ambiguous look direction starts with three or four still mockups made by hacking the lookdev scenes. The art director picks one; the operator is not asked.                                                                                                                              | Briefing and building the first direction that comes to mind, or asking the operator to choose between mockups.                                   | A still costs minutes and a built look costs days. Asking the operator to pick puts a design decision on them, which section 1 says is the swarm's.                                                                                                         |
| Look work is drawn to art.md and the art director's brief on #362, and the look pass compares each `look` pull request with that target as well as with `main`.                                                                                                                                   | Each `look` issue's brief standing on its own, and the look pass comparing with `main` only.                                                      | One target keeps the pieces of the slice drawn as one picture. Comparing with `main` alone says a change is better, not whether it is getting close to what the slice must look like.                                                                       |
| Outcomes come from the simulation, never from a clock or scripted animation. No caps that make effort pointless, and every state on screen is a real sim state.                                                                                                                                   | Scripting the slice's set pieces (the landing, the siege) on a timeline and simulating only the fights.                                           | A scripted outcome does not respond to what chatters do, so their effort means nothing. A state that is only drawn cannot be saved, replayed or tested for determinism.                                                                                     |
| Models: Mythos 5.2 for direction once per artifact, the art director's work, the daily playtest, milestone closing reviews and reviews of determinism, saving and large sim changes. Opus 5.5 at `high` for hard sim or renderer builds and design rounds. Opus 5.5 at `medium` for the rest.     | Effort `high` for every builder and reviewer, and Mythos building where the result is on screen (the first draft of #366).                        | Usage ran out too fast at that allocation. Mythos is kept where its judgement changes the outcome; the bulk of the code is built well at `medium` from a brief that names the approach.                                                                     |
| One strong gate per piece of work. Every pull request, specs and design rounds included, gets one review. A `look` pull request gets one look pass from the art director: the art sign-off, the comparisons with `main` and the look target, and the absolute verdict in one comment.             | A blind watch judge and an art director each looking at every `look` pull request; or letting a spec whose author ran its own critic skip review. | Two Mythos passes per pull request cost twice the quota. A review exemption for self-critiqued specs had no clear owner or test of when it applied, and contradicted "nothing merges unreviewed".                                                           |
| The daily playtest gets the last playtest's capture as a baseline and says better, the same or worse, with the reason. A "worse" is a fix-now `watch` issue listing the merges in between.                                                                                                        | The playtest giving no comparison, so no blind agent ever says the game got worse.                                                                | The art director knows what each change was for, so its "worse" is not blind. One comparison a day by a judge told nothing of the merges catches a regression within a day, at no extra Mythos pass.                                                        |
| The conductor may apply a reviewer's `suggestion` blocks itself, run the gate, and re-squash or rebase a branch for merge. It builds nothing else.                                                                                                                                                | Starting a builder for every fix round and every re-squash.                                                                                       | Applying given lines and re-squashing are not building, and waiting for a builder slot to do them delays the merge.                                                                                                                                         |
| Every brief asks the builder for a design note on the issue before any code: the approach, the files and data touched, and what is left out.                                                                                                                                                      | Builders starting from the brief and explaining the approach only in the pull request.                                                            | A wrong approach caught in a note costs a comment; caught in review it costs the build.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| The conductor does its small chores in batches, and reports one line per merge in the work-cycle thread and a fuller report when a milestone closes.                                                                                                                                              | A ten-line report at the end of every cycle, and chores done one at a time.                                                                       | The operator asked for reports at milestones (#366). A ten-line report per cycle repeats what the pull request already says; one line per merge still shows the work moving.                                                                                |
| Operator messages are user feedback and never gate design, priority or quality. Only deployment and hosting are the operator's. Changes to this agreement go through an issue, a pull request, a review and a decision note, like code, and wait on nobody; the operator can veto after the fact. | Putting some or all changes to the agreement behind the operator's yes.                                                                           | The operator asked "why do you need my approval?" when asked to approve this one. Work that waits on the operator holds a slot for as long as they are away, and the veto after the fact already covers a change they don't want.                           |
