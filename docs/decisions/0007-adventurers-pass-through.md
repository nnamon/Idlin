# 0007: Adventurers walk through each other

Date: 2026-11-27. Raised by the operator: residents shouldn't block each other,
only other things should block them, which also stops a player blocking a
narrow way to annoy the others.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Rejected alternative                                             | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Two adventurers never block each other; they can share a tile while passing. Monsters and adventurers block each other.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Everyone blocks everyone, with walkers routing round each other. | Once residents belong to players, blocking is a way to grief: one resident on the bridge holds the river. Routing round each other in crowds also kept residents jammed or shuffling (12 of 50 stuck in a game day on `main`). |
| A resident walking somewhere still picks a tile nobody stands on or is walking to, and of two stopped on one tile, the next of them to decide `walk_to` moves off, even if sent to that very tile, unless it can reach nowhere free (monsters at both ends of a corridor, say), in which case it stays and the two keep sharing. One that decides to wait or idle stays put, so two can share a tile until one of them walks (often the one that arrived later, whose program decides again as soon as its walk ends). Someone struck down wakes at camp on the nearest tile nobody stands on or is walking to; failing that, on the nearest tile nobody stands on; failing that, where it fell. | Letting everyone stop on the exact tile asked for.               | Without it everyone sent to camp ends on one tile, and a pile of figures can't be read.                                                                                                                                        |
| A blow lands on every enemy on a marked tile.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | One target per tile.                                             | Two adventurers can share a tile now; hitting only one of them would make sharing a way to hide.                                                                                                                               |
