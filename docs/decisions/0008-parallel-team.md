# 0008: The conductor runs three builders and builds nothing itself

Date: 2026-11-27. Decided by the conductor on the operator's issue #233, which
asked for the conductor to orchestrate a team that builds around the clock.
Changes row 1 of [0005](0005-one-long-session.md) (one issue after another),
replaces its rows 2 and 3 (the conductor building and starting its next issue
during a review; at most two child tasks and one heavy container job at a
time), and replaces the optional artist role.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Rejected alternative                                                                                                                     | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The conductor does not build. It keeps up to three builder children running (Opus 5.5, effort `medium`), each in its own worktree on one independent `ready` issue, each opening its own pull request. A finished builder's slot goes to the next independent issue straight away.       | The conductor builds one issue at a time and starts the next while the last is in review, with an optional artist child for `look` work. | The operator asked for it. With the conductor building, only one issue moved at a time and reviews, merges and planning waited on the build. Builders on issues that don't share files can work side by side, and each branch still starts from `main`, so nothing is stacked. |
| The conductor's job is splitting and ordering issues so builders don't collide, writing their briefs, triaging reviews (sending fixes back to the builder or to a fresh one), merging, and keeping `main` green.                                                                         | Builders merge their own pull requests.                                                                                                  | Merges reach `main` one at a time, after a review whose findings the builder should not be the one to dismiss. One merger also sees when two pull requests start to overlap.                                                                                                   |
| At most three builders and at most three reviewers or judges at once. The design child of a `look` issue and the daily digest's watch judge count among the three reviewers or judges.                                                                                                   | Two child tasks of any role, as before; or no limit.                                                                                     | Each builder's pull request needs its own review, and a review waiting for a slot holds up a merge. A fixed cap keeps load and model quota use known in advance; three is the number #233 sets.                                                                                |
| Captures run one at a time across the team: whoever starts one (builder, conductor or digest) checks `docker ps` for a running capture first, and if two start at once the one that notices retries later. Up to six `pnpm gate` runs may overlap, one per builder and one per reviewer. | One heavy container job of either kind at a time.                                                                                        | A capture drives headless Chromium and is the heaviest job the project runs. With three builders and three reviewers each running the gate, one gate at a time would leave most of them waiting. A gate run is lint, typecheck and tests, far lighter than a capture.          |
| A builder squashes the branch to one UTC-dated commit when the conductor says the review is clean; the conductor checks that commit and pushes it to `main` as a fast-forward, without checking out the branch.                                                                          | The conductor checks out the branch and squashes it, as in 0005.                                                                         | The shared checkout stays on `main`. The squash runs in a builder's worktree on a local branch of another name made from the remote branch, so it works even if an old builder's worktree still has the branch checked out.                                                    |
