# 0032: Elements and statuses in the camp world

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #402, slice piece 7, open to veto. Sections 4.2
and 4.3 of [slice.md](../slice.md) give the four damage types, the status
each leaves and the defences. The rules are in `packages/sim/src/status.ts`
and the world applies them in `strike` and `landAttacks`. The world is still
the camp and its regions, with no gear, no landing set down and no monster
levels from the ground, so these choices were made where the spec was silent
or the camp world got in the way.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                | Rejected alternative                                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A monster kind may give a `rank` and a `level`; left out it is level-1 fodder, which hashes as kinds did before.                                                                                        | Waiting for piece 5 to give each monster its level.  | The status time is by rank and an element's line by level, so both have to come from somewhere to be tested in the world. Piece 5 moves the level onto each monster. |
| A region's map carries the landing's `element`, which a generated landing has from its biome. A content map has none.                                                                                   | A world-wide element.                                | When piece 3 sets a landing down it will be a region's map, and the element comes with it; tests stand a world on each biome's landing with a camp at its ramp.      |
| A stagger has no time of its own. It breaks a wind-up if there is one and starts its 2 seconds of lockout either way.                                                                                   | A stagger that lasts the status time.                | 4.2 gives it no effect but the broken wind-up. The lockout is on the actor, so the viewer can draw the broken coil while it runs.                                    |
| A citizen takes an element's status from a monster's part of 5% of its life, and is never staggered.                                                                                                    | Staggering citizens at 5%.                           | With residents at 20 hitpoints the line is 1, and every monster's blow would break a resident's swing. 4.2's paragraph on citizens speaks of the elements' parts.    |
| A status on a citizen lasts 4 seconds.                                                                                                                                                                  | 2 seconds.                                           | A citizen has no rank; 4 seconds is the base the ranks count down from.                                                                                              |
| A burn on a citizen does at least 1 a second.                                                                                                                                                           | A fifth rounded down, which can be 0.                | At 20 hitpoints a part over the line can be under 5, and a burn that is drawn but does nothing reads as a bug.                                                       |
| A burn jumps at its first second, to the nearest monster within a tile that is not burning; a shock arcs to the nearest other monster within 3; ties go to update order. No roll is involved in either. | A seeded pick.                                       | 4.2 says no roll is involved, and the nearest is what a viewer expects to see the burn spread to.                                                                    |
| An arc is a hit of lightning alone, through the second monster's resistance and its own shock, that sets no status and does not arc again.                                                              | Arcs that set shock.                                 | One arc a hit keeps a crowd of shocked monsters from chaining a hit across the plate.                                                                                |
| A freeze's 2-second lockout runs from the thaw. A freeze stops everything the monster was doing: its wind-up, recovery and step stand still.                                                            | Counting from the freeze.                            | From the freeze, the lockout would be over by the thaw on fodder and do nothing.                                                                                     |
| A burn does no experience. Experience still comes from the hits.                                                                                                                                        | Experience for burn damage.                          | Experience counts what a blow took, and a burn is not a blow.                                                                                                        |
| Knockback comes only with a physical part.                                                                                                                                                              | Every hit knocks back.                               | 4.2: "Knockback is physical too." Every blow has a physical part until gear adds elements.                                                                           |
| Version 11 snapshots hash every actor's status. A version 10 snapshot loads with no statuses and keeps its blows, which fit version 10's weapons already.                                               | Calling version 10 wind-ups off as version 9's were. | Nothing about a version 10 blow changed.                                                                                                                             |
