# 0036: No clock on the shield, and a horde that grows

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #396, open to veto. The operator, on the
siege: "How is the lift off time known when you can't calculate how long
the players will fend off the horde monsters from the bubble shield? Are you
just hardcoding this as a scripted animation … instead of actual gameplay
mechanics?" and "Of course the horde strength should grow such that it's
impossible to hold out forever." On the posts: "If it's sufficient for one
gap to be made to force the city to leave then what is the point of other
posts? I would say that it doesn't allow the monsters to pour through a
gap, instead the shield just takes more damage from hits at that location
and the entire shield collapses at once." And earlier, that the siege
should reward strong players who can hold out and gather more loot. The
rules are in [slice.md](../slice.md), sections 1, 2, 3.2 and 5.
[0035](0035-seven-phases-a-shield-and-a-city-that-grows.md)'s rows on the
cycle, the shield, the siege's pay and going aboard follow this note.
