# 0011: Monsters go home when their part of the day ends, and keep off the camp's ground

Date: 2026-11-27. Made in #220 (pull request #248), monsters as content.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Rejected alternative                                                                                | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A monster from the spawn table comes out for the day or for the night, and goes home when that part of the day is over: day ones at 19:00, night ones at 06:00.                                                             | Monsters stay until they are killed, whatever the hour.                                             | Six monsters at most are alive at once. Day monsters that never left would fill the cap, so the night's monsters, which come closer to camp, would rarely get in and night would look like day.                                                                      |
| One goes home only when no adventurer is within eight steps of it and it is not in the middle of a blow.                                                                                                                    | Every monster goes at the stroke of the hour.                                                       | A monster vanishing in the middle of a fight, or with its blow already marked on the ground, reads as a glitch on screen, and a blow that never lands breaks the rule that a marked tile is struck.                                                                  |
| Monsters never stand on or walk onto the camp's `camp` tiles. The world enforces it: their walks, `step_clear`, `flee`, `wander`, spawns, inputs and restored snapshots all keep them off. Their blows still reach onto it. | Leave it to the monster programs, whose "shy of the fire" rules already turn them back before camp. | Downed adventurers wake at camp and rest there for fifteen seconds. A monster that got in, through a program bug or a blow it stepped out of, could stand among them and strike each one as it wakes. A rule in content can be edited away; one in the world cannot. |
| Monsters put in by hand, by an input, have no part of the day and never go home.                                                                                                                                            | They go home at the next dusk or dawn like the rest.                                                | An input is a deliberate act by whoever runs the world, usually a test or a demonstration, and should stay where it was put.                                                                                                                                         |

A version 4 snapshot, from before these rules, is still read. One that holds
more than six monsters, or a monster on or walking onto the camp's ground, is
refused with a message saying which.
