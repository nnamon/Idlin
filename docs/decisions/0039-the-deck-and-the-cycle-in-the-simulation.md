# 0039: The deck and the cycle in the simulation

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #432, slice piece 2a, open to veto. The world
becomes the walking city: its deck, the cycle's beats on their ticks, and a
generated landing set down at legs-plant and taken up at lift-off. The rules
are in [slice.md](../slice.md), sections 1, 2 and 8; the code is
`packages/sim/src/cycle.ts`, `deck.ts` and `world.ts`.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Rejected                                                                             | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A city's ground is worked out from its cycle: the deck in transit, the landing with the hull in it from legs-plant to lift-off, its ramp walkable from 3:20. The snapshot holds the cycle and the landing's input, not the map. | Storing the landing's tiles in the snapshot.                                         | The landing is a pure function of its input, so storing it would only make snapshots large. Generated landings are kept by input, so a restore or a viewer message does not generate one again.                                                                                                                    |
| The landing's map is offset so the deck's tiles have the same world coordinates on both grounds.                                                                                                                                | Moving everyone aboard at each swap.                                                 | Nobody aboard moves when the ground changes, walks and blows in wind-up on the deck carry over, and programs are compiled once against the deck's places.                                                                                                                                                          |
| The deck's edge is not a step: deck and ground are joined only through the ramp, for walks and knockbacks.                                                                                                                      | A deck tile next to the landing's road being walkable both ways.                     | The generator puts the hull on ground that is walkable all round it, so without this a citizen stepped off the hull anywhere and the ramp meant nothing.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Until piece 31, the break comes 2,400 ticks into the siege, or earlier on a `break` input that only tests send. `lift_eta` counts as if the shield broke the moment the front reached the bubble.                               | A short stand-in siege, such as a minute; `lift_eta` counting to the stand-in break. | The issue's test puts breaks at 11:00, 12:50 and 14:20, and the stand-in has to come after all of them; 2,400 ticks is past section 1's longest siege. Reading the earliest break a real shield could have keeps `lift_eta` from ever reading past a real lift-off and gives the stand-in no meaning to a program. |
| A deck tile berths two citizens, and a `walk_to` a deck tile ends on a deck tile with room.                                                                                                                                     | One citizen to a tile, as on the ground.                                             | The first deck is 78 tiles and the city berths 150 (section 1). With one to a tile, about half of a full city stopped on the ground beside the hull at every lift.                                                                                                                                                 |
| At lift-off monsters, marks and arrows go, every flag comes down, and each citizen not on the deck is put on a berth by the hold. One not on the ramp either goes through `strip`, which does nothing until piece 17.           | Leaving citizens where they stood on the landing's ground.                           | Nothing may stand on ground that is gone, and section 8's strip needs one place to happen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Snapshot version 13. Every older one is refused with a message saying it is of the camp world, and the server sets it aside and starts a new city. The migrations for versions 4 to 11 go.                                      | Adopting camp snapshots onto the deck.                                               | The camp's regions, residents and spawn table have nothing they map onto. Nothing is deleted: the old files are kept under `.bad`.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| The camp's clock and firelight syscalls are retired: their numbers stay taken and the compiler and the machine refuse them.                                                                                                     | Removing them, or having them read 0.                                                | Numbers are permanent (syscalls.ts). A sensor that reads 0 would give a fixed clock a meaning again.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| The camp's map, programs, wolf and goblin stay as test fixtures, and a world can still be made on one fixed map with no cycle.                                                                                                  | Rewriting every fight, walk and program test on a landing.                           | Those tests check the rules of fighting and walking, which a fixed map shows as well, and the landing has no monsters until piece 5.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| The live viewer is a plain view, flat tiles and dots, and the camp renderer goes with the camp.                                                                                                                                 | Keeping the camp renderer drawing the deck.                                          | Piece 2b draws the deck and the landing in the look target's renderer (#362), which shares nothing with the camp's.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
