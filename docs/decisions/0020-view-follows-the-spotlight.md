| A pan takes 8 seconds per region, eased in and out (smoothstep), and goes region by region along the exits, slowing to a stop over the camp between two legs. At 8 seconds the fastest frame moves 4.5 pixels at six frames a tick. A stay after each pan left the view on an empty region at night for up to 10 seconds once its resident had walked back (seen in the first capture); the 2-second wait is enough to stop a resident stepping back and forth over an exit from swinging the view. |
