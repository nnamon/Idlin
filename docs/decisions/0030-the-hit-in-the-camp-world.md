# 0030: The hit in the camp world

Date: 2026-11-28. Decided for #383, slice piece 4, open to veto. Section 4.1
of [slice.md](../slice.md) is built as `hit` in `packages/sim/src/hit.ts`,
and every blow in the world goes through it. The world it lands in is still
the camp and its regions, with no gear, no ranks and no armour, so a few
choices had to be made about how the two meet until pieces 2, 5 and 13
replace that world.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                          | Rejected alternative                                                                  | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

| A resident's blow is the base blow of its Melee level, which stands in for Arms, with a crit roll each swing. A monster's blow is its file's `damage`.            | Renaming Melee to Arms, and giving the shipped monsters ranks and levels, now.        | Both change saved worlds, programs and the viewer, and pieces 2 and 5 replace the residents and monsters anyway. The hit is the same function either way.                                                                               |

Now a level-1 resident needs as many blows as before. |
